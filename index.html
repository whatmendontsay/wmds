WMDS Publisher

The private publishing tool behind the What Men Don't Say channel.

What Men Don't Say is a channel of narrated video essays about men's lives: relationships, friendship, work, loneliness and getting older. Watch it at https://www.youtube.com/@what_men_dont_say.

WMDS Publisher is an internal tool used only by the channel's owner. It helps prepare each video and then uploads it, once the owner has reviewed and approved it, to the owner's own channel through the YouTube API Services. It also reads the channel's own performance statistics.

The tool is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, and no other person's account or data is used.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads approved videos to the owner's channel, with title, description, tags, category, the "not made for kids" setting and YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • Sets each video's custom thumbnail and uploads its captions.
  • Reads view, retention and click-through statistics for the owner's own videos (read-only).
